PolicyLens privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

PolicyLens summarises privacy policies and terms of service. This page explains what happens to the information you give it, through the website or the Chrome extension.

What we receive

  • The policy you want summarised. When you click "Analyze this page" in the extension, it reads the text of the page you are on and sends it to our server. On the website, we receive the text, link or PDF you submit.
  • Your IP address. Our server sees it with every request, like any website. We use it only to limit how many analyses one person can run.

The extension reads a page only when you click the button. It never reads pages in the background and never collects your browsing history.

What we do with it

  • The policy text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which writes the summary. Anthropic handles it under its commercial terms and privacy policy, and does not use it to train its models.
  • We do not save the text you send or the summary we return. We have no database and no user accounts.
  • IP addresses are kept in memory for at most 24 hours for rate limiting, and are also in our hosting provider's standard request logs.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone, apart from Anthropic as described above.
  • We don't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Stored on your device

The extension stores one setting in your browser: the address of the PolicyLens server.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: github.com/jefferissjonesoscar-dev/Policylens.
